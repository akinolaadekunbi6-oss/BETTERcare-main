Book an appointment

Choose a virtual or in-person visit. You'll get a confirmation by email with the details and, for virtual visits, your video link closer to the time.

How it works

  1. 01

    Book a slot

    Choose "Virtual" when booking and pick a time that fits your schedule — most slots are available same-day.

  2. 02

    Receive a link

    You'll get a secure video link by email and text 15 minutes before your appointment. No app download needed.

  3. 03

    Join from anywhere on campus

    Join from your room, the library or a study space — you just need a quiet spot and a stable connection.

  4. 04

    Get your plan

    The doctor gives you a treatment plan, e-prescription (sent to the campus pharmacy) or a referral, all summarised in your student health portal.

  5. 05

    Follow up if needed

    If the doctor recommends an in-person exam or test, you'll be offered the next available in-clinic slot before you leave the call.
