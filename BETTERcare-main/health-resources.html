Lab tests

Blood work, urinalysis and rapid tests are ordered by a clinic doctor when needed. Results are sent to your health portal.

Rapid tests

In person

What to expect

1. Talk with a clinician

Book a consultation to discuss your concern. A clinic doctor will decide whether testing is needed and place an order.

2. Follow the instructions

The clinic team will explain where to go and any preparation needed for the specific test.

3. Check your health portal

Test results are sent to your health portal. Your clinician can discuss the results and any next steps with you.
