Services at a glance

General consultations, mental health support, a campus pharmacy, basic lab tests and vaccinations are all handled through the same clinic. Some can be started virtually and continued in person if needed.

See all services

Not sure where to start?

If it's urgent, call the 24/7 nurse triage line rather than booking online a nurse will tell you whether to come in, start a virtual consult, or go to the nearest hospital.

For anything non-urgent, a virtual consult is usually the fastest way to be seen.
