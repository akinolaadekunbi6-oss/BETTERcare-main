Get a prescription

Book a virtual or in person consultation. Your doctor will decide whether medication is appropriate and issue a prescription when needed.

Pick it up on campus

Prescriptions issued by a clinic doctor can be filled directly at the on site campus pharmacy for in person pickup.

Need a renewal?

Prescription renewals may be handled without a full consultation where clinically appropriate. Contact the clinic to ask about your situation.

For medication questions or pickup guidance, contact the clinic team.
