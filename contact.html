Frequently asked questions

Is the clinic free for students?

Consultations are covered by the student health fee included in registration. Lab tests, some vaccines and pharmacy items may carry a small additional cost, shown before you confirm.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Your health record is only accessible to clinic staff directly involved in your care, and is never shared with faculty or administration without your written consent, except where required by law.

Can I be seen without an appointment?

Walk-ins are accepted for urgent same day concerns, but booking ahead virtual or in person means a shorter wait.

Do you see students living off campus?

Yes, all currently enrolled students can use the clinic, regardless of where they live. Virtual consults are available to anyone with an internet connection.

What do I do if I'm having a mental health crisis?

Call the 24/7 nurse triage line immediately, or go to the clinic's emergency bay do not wait for a scheduled appointment. See Contact details for numbers.

Can I get a medical excuse letter for missed classes?

Yes, ask during your consultation virtual or in person and it will be issued through your student health portal.

How soon can I be seen?

Virtual slots are often available same day. In-person visits are usually confirmed within 24 hours, faster for urgent requests.

Can I choose a specific doctor?

You can mention a preference in the reason field we'll try to match you, but it isn't guaranteed depending on availability. See who's on staff under Doctors.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Use the link in your confirmation email, or contact the clinic directly details are on this page.
