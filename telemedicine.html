Good for a virtual consult

Colds, flu-like symptoms, allergies, skin concerns, minor injuries, prescription renewals, mental health check-ins, and follow-ups on an existing treatment plan.

Needs an in-person visit instead

Anything requiring a physical exam, lab work, vaccination, suturing, or anything you'd describe as urgent or severe. For those, book an in-clinic appointment or use the emergency line.
